My Journey

I am a Computer Science master's student and researcher at the University of Southern California, passionate about machine learning and natural language processing, with a particular focus on deep representation learning. My research centers on developing novel neural architectures and learning objectives for theoretical machine learning and for solving problems in scientific and medical domains. In parallel, my NLP work is driven by mechanistic interpretability and brain-inspired mechanisms aimed at better understanding and improving large language models.
