WHO I AM

My Journey

I am a machine learning researcher and engineer with a background in Computer Engineering and an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California. My work spans deep learning theory, bioinformatics, and medical AI, with a focus on multiview and multimodal learning, generative and probabilistic models, and graph neural networks—leading to peer-reviewed publications in Nature Communications (2025) and IEEE Access (2024). More recently, I have focused on large language models, particularly alignment, safety, and mechanistic interpretability. My research explores circuit-level understanding and behavioral steering of LLMs, with ongoing work on multi-layer steering methods inspired by transformer circuit discovery. In parallel, I build end-to-end ML systems across multimodal RAG, LLM evaluation and benchmarking, and deep research agent pipelines for scientific discovery. I’m particularly interested in bridging theory and real-world systems—taking insights from representation learning and interpretability into practical, scalable AI systems.
